WHO WE ARE

Sourcing quality agricultural products from India.

AAA Internationals is an India-focused agricultural export and trading company engaged in sourcing and supplying quality agricultural products to international buyers.

We work with carefully selected farmers, growers, processors, packhouses and suppliers across India to source products according to customer requirements.

Our product portfolio includes fresh fruits and vegetables, seeds and spices, with a particular focus on products such as Nashik Red Onions, G9 Grade A Bananas, Pomegranates, G4 Green Chillies, Fresh Ginger, Chia Seeds and Turmeric.

Our objective is to build long-term relationships with international importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers and food businesses by providing reliable sourcing, transparent communication and consistent export coordination.

OUR APPROACH

Quality sourcing,
end to end.

From the first farm interaction to final delivery, every step is coordinated around the buyer's requirements.

01

Quality

02
