OUR EXPORT PROCESS

From inquiry to documents.

Each order follows a defined sequence so the buyer always knows what happens next.

  1. Buyer Inquiry

    Buyer sends product, quantity, specifications and destination.

  2. Requirement Confirmation

    We confirm grade, size, packing, quantity, shipment terms and documentation requirements.

  3. Quotation

    Commercial quotation / proforma invoice is provided.

  4. Order Confirmation

    Buyer confirms the order and agreed commercial terms.

  5. Sourcing & Quality Check

    Product is sourced and inspected according to agreed specifications.

  6. Packing

    Product is packed according to agreed packaging requirements.

  7. Documentation

    Required export and shipment documents are prepared.

  8. Container Loading

    Cargo is loaded and shipment arrangements are completed.

  9. Shipment

    Cargo is dispatched to the agreed destination.

  10. Shipping Documents

    Required shipping documents are provided according to agreed terms.

We do not claim a fixed number of countries served. Actual destination availability is confirmed product-by-product and shipment-by-shipment.
